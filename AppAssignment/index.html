Now getting to the point of how to play the game, well its pretty simple actually. All the movement of the pieces are grid locked

  • To move items from one location to another
    1. Tap on a piece.
    2. Tap another location.
    3. Tap on the ARROW icon
    4. Wait for the thing to move

  • To rotate items
    1. Tap on a piece.
    2. Tap on the ROTATING icon
    3. Wait for the thing to rotate

  • To move around
    1. Tap and Drag anywhere on screen.
    2. A yellow colored ball would apear under the finger, its very transparent so hard to see.
    3. Move away from the original location of the tap
    4. The direction your finger drags will be the direction the camera moves

  • To zoom in or out
    1. Tap with 2 fingers and Drag anywhere on screen.
    2. Bring your fingers closer to zoom in
    3. Bring your fingers farther apart to zoom out

  • To check howmuch time left
    1. Click on the stopwatch on the top right

This is pretty much it honestly, if I have missed something this is simple enough that you can figure it out.
